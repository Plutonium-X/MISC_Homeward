# Homeward Bound

This mod allows you to send your companions back to the mainland while you are in Brynnlaw. You can do the same when you are in the Pocket Plane's antechamber.

You can also summon them back and/or view their epilogues even if they are no longer part of your party.

The mod also includes a shop in Brynnlaw.
